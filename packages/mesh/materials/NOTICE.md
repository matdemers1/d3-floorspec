# Material library — sources and licence

The maps in this directory are the material library's (FLR-T-13.6, `@floorspec/mesh/materials`):
each item's base colour (`*.albedo.jpg`), tangent-space normal, +Y up (`*.normal.jpg`), and
occlusion-roughness-metalness (`*.orm.jpg`, occlusion in red, roughness in green, metalness in
blue), and in `previews/` a picker's swatch of each.

They are derived from textures published by [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) and
[ambientCG](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain
dedication). No attribution is required; it is given here anyway, with thanks. Six sets of maps —
standing-seam roofing, lap and board-and-batten siding, subway and zellige tile, and the paints'
roller stipple — are drawn by `scripts/prepare-materials.py` from seeded noise, and are dedicated to
the public domain under CC0 1.0 too.

Each original was downloaded at 2K (a Poly Haven texture's colour, OpenGL normal and packed
ambient-occlusion/roughness/metal JPEGs; an ambientCG material's 2K-JPG archive) from the URL in
`SOURCES.json`, which records its SHA-256, and prepared by `scripts/prepare-materials.py`: scaled to
1K (2K for the hero materials — white oak plank, oak herringbone, terrazzo, marble and red brick)
across a 3 × 3 tiling so its edges still meet, turned so a plank or a course runs along the
surface's `s`, a limewash, a linen and a concrete given a colour of their own, and written as
JPEGs. `SOURCES.json` records every prepared file's digest and length; `src/materials/data.ts`
carries the same, and the items' real-world sizes — Poly Haven's measured `dimensions`, or for an
ambientCG material, counted from what its image shows.

To prepare them again:

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install pillow==11.3.0 numpy==2.3.2
.venv/bin/python -I packages/mesh/scripts/prepare-materials.py <cache-dir>
```

The originals are downloaded into `<cache-dir>` and checked against the digests recorded here;
the same originals give the same files.
